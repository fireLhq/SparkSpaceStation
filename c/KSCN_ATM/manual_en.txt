Welcome to KSCN Bank ATM system. Please follow the instructions.
Select language at startup: Press 1 for Chinese, 2 for English.
Login requires a 9-digit account and a 6-digit numeric password.
System exits automatically after 3 consecutive wrong passwords.
Frozen accounts cannot log in or perform any transactions.
Balance inquiry displays CNY, USD, and EUR accounts.
Deposit limit: Max 20000 per time, must be multiples of 100.
Deposit amount must be positive. Balance updates immediately.
Withdrawal limit: Max 20000 per time, must be multiples of 100.
Withdrawal fails if the balance in chosen currency is low.
Transfer requires valid account and name. Self-transfer denied.
Transfer limit: Max 5000000, allows up to 2 decimal places.
Transfer fails if the target account is frozen.
Forex limit: Max 20000. Paid currency calculated by rate.
Forex fails if payment balance is low. Try another currency.
Records and schedules appear on the right. Press any key to return.
Apply for amounts above 20k up to 2M, or above 5M up to 100M for transfers.
Reservation date must be today or later. Use YYYY MM DD.
Reset pwd requires old pwd. New pwd must be 6 digits.
Account freeze sets status to frozen and exits system.

001#How can I change language?#Press 1 for Chinese or 2 for English on system startup.
002#What is input format for login?#Account needs 9 digits and password requires 6 digits.
003#What if I type wrong password?#Three failed login attempts or three incorrect current passwords during a reset will close the program.
004#What to do if account is frozen?#Frozen accounts cannot log in. To restore a demo account, exit and set its status to 1 in user.txt.
005#Which currency balances can I see?#You can check CNY, USD, and EUR balances in real time.
006#What are the limits for deposit?#Max limit is 20000 per time, must be multiples of 100.
007#What are limits for withdrawal?#Max limit is 20000 per time, must be multiples of 100.
008#What info is required for transfer?#Target 9-digit account and registered name are required.
009#Does transfer support decimals?#Yes, max limit is 5000000 with up to 2 decimal places.
010#Can I transfer money to myself?#No, transferring to your current logged-in account is blocked.
011#Can I transfer to frozen account?#No, system will stop trade and show target account is frozen.
012#What are current exchange rates?#CNY rate is 1.0, USD rate is 7.20, and EUR rate is 7.80.
013#What if payment balance is low?#System alerts low balance. You can change payment currency.
014#What is maximum Forex limit?#Max purchase limit for target currency is 20000 per trade.
015#How can I view transaction logs?#Select 06 Record in menu to view history on the right.
016#When to use Big-Amount Apply?#Apply when over limits: 20k-2M for cash, 5M-100M for transfer.
017#What is valid reservation date?#Date cannot be past. Please use YYYY MM DD format.
018#How to cancel my reservation?#Enter 0 0 0 during date input step to cancel application.
019#Can I cancel password reset?#Yes, type exit!! during password input to cancel anytime.
